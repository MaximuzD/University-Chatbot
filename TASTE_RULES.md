# Design Rules: Anti-Slop & Taste Skill Guidelines

Adapted from `Leonxlnx/taste-skill` for Horizon University Student Portal.

## 1. Eliminate Generic AI Tropes ("Slop")
- **NO ungrounded gradients**: Do not use arbitrary purple-to-indigo or neon gradient overlays behind standard cards or buttons.
- **NO resting heavy drop shadows**: Prefer crisp 1px borders with subtle surface value shifts over blurry, dramatic shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px ...`).
- **NO redundant badge rows / repetitive eyebrow tags**: Don't put "Official Record", "Active", "Verified" on every single box. Keep badges purposeful.
- **NO fake rounded pill overkill**: Use intentional border radii (8px–12px) instead of bloated 24px–32px bubbles everywhere.

## 2. Intentional Editorial Typography & Layout
- **Clear typographic hierarchy**: Headlines must be concise (two lines maximum), high contrast, with clear optical sizing.
- **Generous negative space & breathing room**: Let tables, cards, and reading zones breathe with disciplined padding rather than cramming UI into tiny segmented boxes.
- **Authentic institutional palette**: Horizon Blue (`#0f2b5c` / `#1e3a8a`), crisp neutral backgrounds (`#f8fafc` / `#ffffff`), slate typography (`#0f172a`, `#475569`), with functional amber/emerald states that aren't neon.
